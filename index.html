THE PRACTICE

I build the products and operating systems that let ambitious technology survive contact with reality.

That means turning complex, high-consequence work into clear controls, durable workflows, and teams that can move fast without creating tomorrow’s incident.

I’ve done it across cryptography, digital assets, foreign exchange, and the control environments behind Bullish, American Express, and Truist—building the backbone for asset controls, onboarding, diligence, risk, and execution at scale.

That range matters because I know what it takes to make frontier technology useful inside organizations where failure is expensive, regulated, and very real.

Now I’m taking that discipline to the next frontier: AI and data-center operations. Through Drytide Labs, I’m building, testing, and documenting the systems that make powerful new infrastructure practical: agents, automation, observability, workflows, and the operating models around them.
