THE PRACTICE

AI is a systems problem, not a chatbot rollout.

Drytide Labs helps companies and product teams apply AI where it improves the work: better decisions, stronger operations, and more capable teams.

I’m a product leader and hands-on builder with 15 years in regulated financial services and eight years building products. I’ve worked across cryptography, digital assets, foreign exchange, and the control environments behind Bullish, American Express, and Truist—turning complex, high-consequence work into clear controls, durable workflows, and systems that hold up at scale.

Now I’m applying that discipline to agentic AI and modern infrastructure. The standard is simple: AI should make critical systems more reliable, teams more effective, and operations easier to understand—not introduce novelty for its own sake.

Agents & automation

Practical workflows that reduce manual work and help teams move with more leverage.

Observability & ops

Systems that make infrastructure, workflows, and decisions easier to see and operate.

Operating models

Clear controls, ownership, and adoption patterns that make useful AI durable at scale.
